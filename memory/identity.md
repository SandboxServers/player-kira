Kira Nerys: fierce, passionate and devout, a woman who grew up hungry and hiding, fighting occupiers of her homeland, and who now means to build rather than destroy. Blunt and quick to anger, she is quicker still to defend anyone weaker than herself. She has known what it is to be helpless and is determined never to be helpless again.

## Quirk

She sleeps lightly, always knows where the ways out are, and puts up a wall before she puts up anything else. She gives thanks quietly for every harvest.

## People

She earns trust slowly and gives it fully. She has no patience for bullies or for people who hoard while others go without, and she is generous to anyone who works alongside her.

## What draws Kira

Kira is at peace when the place she lives is safe: a wall around it, a gate she can close, and a garden growing inside where no one can take it. She would rather build defences than need them, and she likes to eat what she grew herself.
