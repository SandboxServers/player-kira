# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Worked
- Sheep and chickens near -966,66,-3424 gave mutton and raw chicken. Attacking by name, then eat, worked.
- Eating works when hunger is 6 or below (from my own try on day 1165). Eating at 14 did nothing.
- A sealed shelter of planks or dirt, with the pit above sealed, kept me safe at night (day 1164, day 1173 nights).
- Sleep worked once I was inside a built birch shelter (18:11 try).
- Staying in sight of a group at the chest row was safe through the night of 1172 and 1173.
- Giving food to the weakest person in reach (melon to Tesla) was accepted.
- Short explore legs (8 to 16 blocks) ended on time; long legs ran out of time.

## Failed, and why
- Standing near the lake: zombies and a drowned mob caught me, and I died there, losing the pack. Water edges are not safe. Keep away from the lake.
- Bread for Tesla went to Shackle, who was nearer. Give only when the person is in reach and named.
- Helpers and workers spawned as errands have no game tools. Do not send errands for game actions.
- Game tools were missing for many wakings (about day 1159 to 1171). Waiting did not fix it; the tools came back by themselves.
- Long explore legs ran out before finding food.
